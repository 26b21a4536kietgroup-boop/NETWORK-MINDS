Problem Statement

A social network of 5 people with influence weights is represented using an influence propagation matrix A. Using Aⁿ via the Cayley-Hamilton theorem, determine the influence after n rounds of discussion, identify the most influential person, and determine whether everyone converges to the same opinion.
